# Client Guest Registration Authority for Windows AD and Zero Touch PKI

Provisioning certificates to users and devices in a Windows environment often involves manual requests and separate identity checks. The Guest Registration Authority (Guest RA) by Secur Solutions Group streamlines provisioning and management of digital identities, using Zero Touch PKI as the certificate authority and integrating with Windows Active Directory (AD).

**Value Highlights**

- **Streamline provisioning**: Reduce manual steps to issue certificates to clients in your Windows environment.
- **Use your existing directory**: Base registration on identities already managed in Windows AD.
- **Issue from Zero Touch PKI**: Get certificates from the Zero Touch PKI hosted certificate authority.
- **Manage identities centrally**: Keep provisioning and management of digital identities in one process.

**How it Works**

Guest RA acts as a registration authority between Windows AD and Zero Touch PKI. It uses Active Directory to identify the requester and requests the certificate from Zero Touch PKI. Contact Secur Solutions Group for availability and setup.

**Solution Components**

- Zero Touch PKI
- Windows Active Directory
- Guest RA by Secur Solutions Group
