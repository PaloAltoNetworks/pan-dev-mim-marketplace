# RSA Archer GRC

Security, operations and compliance teams need actionable information to reduce risk, avoid outages and stay compliant, but with little visibility into keys and certificates it is hard to prevent problems before they happen. Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted shares its events with RSA Archer GRC, so your teams can track certificate risk and drive remediation through the governance, risk and compliance processes they already use.

**Value Highlights**

- **Inform your teams**: Give security, operations and compliance teams visibility and updates on keys and certificates.
- **Identify issues and risks** early enough to remediate them.
- **Operationalize remediation**: Run certificate-related processes, compliance and remediation through your enterprise GRC platform.
- **Share the full activity record**: Send key and certificate lifecycle events, administrator activity and workflow approvals to Archer.
- **Spot anomalies**: Analyze activity and operations to find events that need attention.

**How it Works**

Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted forwards its key and certificate lifecycle events, administrator activity and workflow approvals to RSA Archer GRC. Archer then uses those events in its risk, compliance and remediation workflows.

**Solution Components**

- Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted
- RSA Archer GRC

[Archer](https://www.archerirm.com/), formerly part of RSA Security, provides integrated risk management and GRC software.
