# DigiCert CertCentral

DigiCert's CertCentral ensures complete security for public trust certificates, and for
your organization's roots of trust for private PKI. Venafi's native integration lets you
add CertCentral directly as a certificate authority, giving you full lifecycle automation
— request, issuance, renewal, and revocation — without custom scripting.

**SOLUTION HIGHLIGHTS**
- Native, built-in CA
- Full certificate lifecycle automation from request through revocation
- OV and EV certificate support out of the box
- Built and supported directly by Venafi
