# DigiCert CertCentral

DigiCert CertCentral issues and manages publicly trusted TLS/SSL certificates. Certificate Manager, SaaS includes DigiCert CertCentral as a built-in certificate authority (CA), so teams can request and renew DigiCert certificates under central policy without custom scripting.

**Value Highlights**

- **Use a built-in CA**: Add DigiCert CertCentral as a CA connection in Certificate Manager, SaaS with no scripts or extra software.
- **Automate the lifecycle**: Handle request, issuance, renewal and revocation from one place.
- **Issue OV and EV certificates**: Use DigiCert's Organization Validated and Extended Validation certificate products.
- **Choose your region**: Connect to DigiCert's United States or European Union servers.
- **Get support from one vendor**: The integration is built and supported by Venafi.

**How it Works**

An administrator adds DigiCert as a certificate authority in Certificate Manager, SaaS using DigiCert CertCentral API credentials and sets a renewal window. Request policies map to DigiCert certificate products, and Certificate Manager, SaaS sends certificate requests to CertCentral and retrieves the issued certificates. For DV certificates, use an ACME CA connection instead. See the Documentation tab for setup.

**Solution Components**

- Certificate Manager, SaaS
- DigiCert CertCentral account with pre-purchased units or post-use billing
