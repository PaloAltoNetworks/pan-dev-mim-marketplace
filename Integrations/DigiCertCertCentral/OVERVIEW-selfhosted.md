# DigiCert CertCentral

DigiCert's CertCentral ensures complete security for public trust certificates, and for
your organization's roots of trust for private PKI. DigiCert CertCentral is available as
a native, built-in CA type in Certificate Manager Self-Hosted, giving you full lifecycle
automation without custom scripting.

**SOLUTION HIGHLIGHTS**
- Native, built-in CA
- Full certificate lifecycle automation from request through revocation
- OV and EV certificate support out of the box
- Built and supported directly by Venafi

**Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted is the product formerly known as Venafi Trust Protection Platform**
