# D-TRUST Digital Certificates using Managed PKI

As enterprise encryption grows, organizations need a stable, reliable certificate authority (CA) combined with strong machine identity management. Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted and D-TRUST work together to automate the full lifecycle of trusted digital certificates, giving organizations visibility and control over their machine identities.

**Value Highlights**

- **Gain full visibility**: See the demand, usage and workflow of D-TRUST certificate services.
- **Automate the lifecycle**: Handle request and issuance through renewal and revocation without manual steps.
- **Enforce policies**: Apply automated workflow and detailed logging to every certificate.
- **Protect machine identities**: Use trusted, publicly rooted D-TRUST certificates.
- **Reduce outages and risk**: Eliminate manual certificate management.

**How it Works**

Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted connects to D-TRUST Managed PKI as a certificate authority. Organizations can then request, issue, install, renew and monitor certificates issued through D-TRUST Managed PKI across their environments, with policy and workflow applied by Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted.

**Solution Components**

- Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted
- D-TRUST Managed PKI

**About D-TRUST**

[D-TRUST GmbH](https://www.d-trust.net/en), a company of the Bundesdruckerei Group, is a trusted certification authority and qualified trust service provider under the European eIDAS regulation. Through its Managed PKI services and Certificate Service Manager (CSM) platform, D-TRUST issues digital certificates, including TLS/SSL, S/MIME and machine certificates, to secure data transmission, email and machine-to-machine communication.

**Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted is the product formerly known as Venafi Trust Protection Platform**
