# D-TRUST Digital Certificates using Managed PKI

Venafi and D-TRUST work together to automate the full lifecycle of trusted digital certificates, giving organizations visibility and control over their machine identities.

  

**SOLUTION HIGHLIGHTS**

- **Gain full visibility **into the demand, usage and workflow of D-TRUST certificate services
- **Automate the lifecycle **from request and issuance through renewal and revocation
- **Enforce policies **with automated workflow and detailed logging
- **Protect machine identities **with trusted, publicly rooted D-TRUST certificates
- **Reduce outages and risk **by eliminating manual certificate management

  

**SOLUTION OVERVIEW**

Due to the rapid rise in enterprise encryption and shifting market dynamics, the need for a stable and reliable certificate authority (CA) coupled with strong machine identity management continues to escalate. Venafi’s visibility and automation protects machine identities that are integrated with D-TRUST digital certificates, enabling organizations to request, issue, install, renew and monitor certificates issued through D-TRUST Managed PKI across their environments.

**ABOUT D-TRUST**

D-TRUST GmbH, a company of the Bundesdruckerei Group, is a trusted certification authority and qualified trust service provider under the European eIDAS regulation. Through its Managed PKI services and Certificate Service Manager (CSM) platform, D-TRUST issues a wide range of digital certificates—including TLS/SSL, S/MIME and machine certificates—to secure data transmission, email and machine-to-machine communication.

**Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted is the product formerly known as Venafi Trust Protection Platform**
