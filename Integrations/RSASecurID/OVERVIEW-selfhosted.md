# RSA SecurID

Administrator accounts are prime targets for attackers, and the accounts that control certificate management are especially valuable. Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted can use RSA SecurID authentication to protect user and administrator sign-in, so access to certificate management follows the same two-factor standard as your other critical security services.

Your organization's policy, hierarchy and authentication are already established and used across applications. Using SecurID with Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted makes it easy to set up secure administrator access that follows your directory hierarchy and reuses your existing authentication.

**Value Highlights**

- **Stay secure** by enforcing two-factor authentication.
- **Use your enterprise standard** RSA authentication.
- **Support all RSA SecurID form factors** your users already carry.
- **Protect every level** of Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted users and administrators.
- **Reuse your existing RSA Authentication Agent** instead of deploying new infrastructure.

**How it Works**

Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted works with your existing RSA Authentication Agent. When users and administrators sign in, they are prompted for their SecurID credentials, which are verified by your RSA SecurID service before access is granted.

**Solution Components**

- Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted
- RSA SecurID with RSA Authentication Agent
