# AWS CloudFront

Amazon AWS accelerates your applications with one of the world's largest content delivery networks. Venafi makes it easy to automate the use HTTPS so you can operate efficiently, securely and protect your content in the cloud.
#### 
**SOLUTION HIGHLIGHTS**
- **Scale the use of HTTPS** without difficult setup or maintenance
- **Retain agility** across clouds and infrastructure 
- **Eliminate outages** with continuous key and certificate updates 
- **Reduce the risk** of security incidents stemming from improperly handled keys 
- **Secure, automated** distribution of keys and certificates
- **Fully automated** issuance and renewal 
- **Agility and control** over keys after Amazon import 
- **ACM integration** works with Amazon Certificate Manager
**SOLUTION OVERVIEW**
Amazon AWS CloudFront is a global content delivery network (CDN) service that securely delivers data, videos, applications, and APIs to your viewers with low latency and high transfer speeds. CloudFront is integrated with AWS – both physical locations that are directly connected to the AWS global infrastructure, as well as other AWS services. Venafi makes it easy to use HTTPS so you can operate securely and protect your content in the cloud.
