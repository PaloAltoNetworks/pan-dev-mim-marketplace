# Sectigo Certificate Manager

The fast growth in HTTPS requires organizations to gain full visibility, automation and control over machine identities. Venafi enables customers to automate the full certificate lifecycle when using Sectigo certificate services.

####   

**SOLUTION HIGHLIGHTS**

- **Automate **the full certificate lifecycle from issuance to revocation
- **Gain full visibility** into the demand, usage and workflow of certificate services
- **Enforce policies** with automated workflow and detailed logging
- **Easily track workflow approvals**, authorization and authentication
- **Supports full lifecycle **from request, issuance and renewal to revocation
- **Enables complete process **for configurable workflow approval and authorization
- **Works with the native **Sectigo API interface

  

**SOLUTION OVERVIEW**

Due to the rapid rise in enterprise encryption, the need for certificate authority (CA) agility and machine identity protection continues to escalate. Venafi’s visibility and automation protects machine identities that are integrated with trusted Sectigo digital certificates. Venafi and Sectigo are recognized for providing leading innovation and expertise for complete machine identity management and secure server environments—empowering customers to expand enterprise-wide encryption initiatives for more business applications in more places.

**Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted is the product formerly known as Venafi Trust Protection Platform**
