# HP JetAdvantage Security Manager

HP JetAdvantage Security Manager delivers policy-based, automated security compliance for HP printing and imaging fleets. Integrated with Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted over SCEP, it automates the enrollment, deployment, and renewal of identity (ID) certificates on HP devices so printers stay compliant without manual certificate handling.

  

**SOLUTION HIGHLIGHTS**

- **Automate certificate enrollment **for HP printers and imaging devices using SCEP
- **Reduce operational overhead **by removing manual, per-device certificate installation
- **Prevent outages **with automated renewal and replacement before certificates expire or are revoked
- **Strengthen compliance **through policy-based assessment and remediation across the fleet
- **Centralize trust **by issuing device identities from Venafi’s managed certificate authorities

  

**SOLUTION OVERVIEW**

HP JetAdvantage Security Manager continuously assesses HP devices against a defined security policy and remediates settings that fall out of compliance, including device certificates. By enrolling through the SCEP service exposed by Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted, Security Manager requests and installs ID certificates on managed printers and automatically renews or replaces them as they near expiration or are revoked. This lets organizations extend Venafi’s certificate lifecycle automation and visibility to their HP print fleet, ensuring every device carries a valid, policy-compliant identity.

**ABOUT HP**

HP JetAdvantage Security Manager is HP’s policy-based security compliance solution for HP printing and imaging environments. It enables administrators to define a single security policy, apply it across the fleet, and automatically detect and remediate drift, helping enterprises protect endpoints, data, and documents at scale.

**Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted is the product formerly known as Venafi Trust Protection Platform**
