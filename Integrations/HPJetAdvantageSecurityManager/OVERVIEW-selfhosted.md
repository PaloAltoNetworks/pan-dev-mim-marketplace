# HP JetAdvantage Security Manager

Printers and imaging devices need valid identity certificates, but installing and renewing them by hand on every device does not scale across a large fleet. HP JetAdvantage Security Manager delivers policy-based, automated security compliance for HP printing and imaging fleets. Integrated with Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted over SCEP, it automates the enrollment, deployment and renewal of identity (ID) certificates on HP devices, so printers stay compliant without manual certificate handling.

**Value Highlights**

- **Automate certificate enrollment**: Enroll HP printers and imaging devices for certificates over SCEP.
- **Reduce operational overhead**: Remove manual, per-device certificate installation.
- **Prevent outages**: Renew and replace certificates automatically before they expire or after they are revoked.
- **Strengthen compliance**: Apply policy-based assessment and remediation across the fleet.
- **Centralize trust**: Issue device identities through certificate authorities managed by Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted.

**How it Works**

HP JetAdvantage Security Manager continuously assesses HP devices against a defined security policy and remediates settings that fall out of compliance, including device certificates. It enrolls through the SCEP service exposed by Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted to request and install ID certificates on managed printers, and renews or replaces them as they near expiration or are revoked. This extends certificate lifecycle automation and visibility to your HP print fleet, so every device carries a valid, policy-compliant identity.

**Solution Components**

- Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted with its SCEP service
- HP JetAdvantage Security Manager
- HP printers and imaging devices

**About HP**

HP JetAdvantage Security Manager is [HP](https://www.hp.com/)'s policy-based security compliance solution for HP printing and imaging environments. Administrators define a single security policy, apply it across the fleet, and automatically detect and remediate drift.
