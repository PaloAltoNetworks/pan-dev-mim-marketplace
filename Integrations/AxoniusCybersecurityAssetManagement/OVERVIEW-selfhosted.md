# Axonius Cybersecurity Asset Management

Axonius delivers a complete and correlated asset inventory, allowing customers to easily identify devices that aren't protected with Venafi.

  

**SOLUTION HIGHLIGHTS**

- **Reduce cyber attack surface **by identifying devices missing on Venafi or with expired certificates seen by Venafi
- **Gain insightful certificate data **that delivers a complete risk profile of any given asset
- **Certificate to device correlation **for understanding which devices are known to Venafi and detail their certificate status
- **Continuously identify devices** with missing or expired certificates using Axonius

  

**SOLUTION OVERVIEW**

Getting a complete and up-to-date asset inventory is the first step to understanding and reducing the cyber attack surface. Without a complete inventory, IT and Security teams struggle to identify which devices are protected with critical security controls, and which devices are configured correctly.

  

Axonius Cybersecurity Asset Management aggregates, correlates, and deduplicates asset data from hundreds of sources to give IT and Security teams a single source of truth into their assets. With a complete inventory, teams can understand their security control coverage, including the ability to continuously identify devices that are not protected by Venafi.

  

**HOW IT WORKS**

- Connect the Venafi adapter on the Axonius platform according to our guide here: [https://docs.axonius.com/docs/venafi](https://docs.axonius.com/docs/venafi)
- Once connected, Axonius will fetch data from Venafi and correlate it known devices already seen by Axonius
- Write queries in Axonius using the no-code Axonius Query Wizard to identify devices known to Venafi, devices unknown to Venafi, and more
- Automate action: use any saved query as a trigger to automate over 115 pre-built actions using the Axonius Enforcement Center
