# Axonius Cybersecurity Asset Management

A complete, current asset inventory is the first step to reducing the attack surface. Without one, IT and security teams struggle to tell which devices are protected by critical security controls and which are configured correctly.

Axonius Cybersecurity Asset Management aggregates, correlates and deduplicates asset data from hundreds of sources into a single source of truth. With the Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted adapter, teams can see which devices are known to Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted, which are missing, and which have expired certificates.

**Value Highlights**

- **Reduce the attack surface**: Identify devices that are missing from Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted or that have expired certificates.
- **Gain certificate insight**: Add certificate data to the risk profile of any asset.
- **Correlate certificates to devices**: See which devices are known to Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted and the status of their certificates.
- **Monitor continuously**: Keep finding devices with missing or expired certificates as the environment changes.
- **Automate the response**: Trigger pre-built actions from any saved query in the Axonius Enforcement Center.

**How it Works**

The Venafi adapter in the Axonius platform fetches data from Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted and correlates it with devices Axonius already knows about. Teams then use the no-code Axonius Query Wizard to find devices known or unknown to Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted, and can use any saved query as a trigger for more than 115 pre-built actions in the Axonius Enforcement Center. See the [Axonius Venafi adapter guide](https://docs.axonius.com/docs/venafi) for setup.
