# F5 Distributed Cloud (XC)

**Seamless Integration for Unparalleled Cybersecurity and Performance** Securing web applications and managing Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) pose significant challenges for enterprises, especially in complex, distributed environments. The strategic partnership between Venafi and F5 addresses these challenges head-on by offering an integrated solution that combines Certificate Manager with F5 Distributed Cloud's advanced application services. This collaboration ensures organizations not only benefit from robust security but also enjoy enhanced agility, superior performance, and comprehensive protection for application delivery, in any environment. **Tailored for Organizations Seeking Comprehensive Cybersecurity Solutions** By automating key and certificate management the solution significantly reduces the risk of application downtime due to expired certificates, increases visibility and control over PKI, and ensures all keys and certificates are current and compliant with policies. F5 enhances this offering by providing a suite of solutions designed to facilitate secure, AI-driven application delivery across various environments, including cloud, data centers, and edge locations. The combined power of Venafi and F5 not only secures communications and digital credentials but also offers flexibility, compliance, and scalability for web application delivery across SaaS, data center(s), and cloud infrastructures.

**Value Highlights:**

- **Automate Key and Certificate Management:** Specifically tailored for the F5 Distributed Cloud environment, enhancing efficiency and security.
- **Minimize Risk of Application Downtime:** By ensuring certificates never expire unnoticed, keeping applications online and operational.
- **Increase Visibility and Control:** Across all applications, providing comprehensive management and oversight.
- **Ensure Compliance:** All keys and certificates are kept up to date, aligning with the organization's security policy and compliance requirements.

**How it Works**

Certificate Manager services integrate with F5 Distributed Cloud to automate the lifecycle management of keys and certificates within F5 Distributed Cloud applications, ensuring they are valid and compliant with the organization's security policy.

**Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted is the product formerly known as Venafi Trust Protection Platform**

**Certificate Manager, SaaS is the product formerly known as Venafi TLS Protect Cloud**
