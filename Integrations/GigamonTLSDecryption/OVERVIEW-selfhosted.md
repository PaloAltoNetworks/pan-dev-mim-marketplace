# Gigamon TLS Decryption

Invalid or expired machine identities allow encrypted threats to slip by undetected. Organizations can leverage Venafi to manage the lifecycle of machine identities in use by Gigamon, automating the delivery of machine identities and limiting gaps in traffic inspection during the certificate renewal process.

  

**SOLUTION HIGHLIGHTS**

- **Get started fast **with existing key and certificate inventory
- **Maximize inspection **with full key and certificate discovery
- **Maintain inspection levels **with fully automated lifecycle and key distribution
- **Increased performance **of inspection with automated distribution of keys and certificates
- **Automated distribution **of replacement keys when new keys are generated

  

**SOLUTION OVERVIEW**

The inability to decrypt traffic at vital ingress and egress points poses great risk to an organization. Invalid or expired machine identities allow threats to go undetected. Organizations can leverage the Venafi Platform to manage the life cycle of machine identities in use by Gigamon, giving them the ability to detect threats that are hiding in encrypted traffic. The result is automated delivery of machine identities, which limits gaps in traffic inspection during the certificate renewal process.
