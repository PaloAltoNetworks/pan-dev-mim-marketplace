# Code Signing for Device Authority KeyScaler

IoT software and firmware updates are a common way for attackers to reach devices and data. A compromised update can lead to data loss, damage to brand reputation, compliance penalties and theft of intellectual property.

Device Authority KeyScaler and CodeSign Protect in Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted together provide an end-to-end solution for signing software and securely delivering updates to IoT devices.

**Value Highlights**

- **Block unauthorized updates**: Reduce the risk of attackers stealing intellectual property or sensitive data through rogue updates.
- **Protect apps and data**: Defend devices against malware and hijacking.
- **Verify every update**: Confirm the source and validity of each software update.
- **Authenticate every request**: Approve no request to a device until KeyScaler verifies the validity of its machine identity, using tokenized authentication.

**How it Works**

Software updates are signed with CodeSign Protect in Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted, which keeps signing keys protected and applies signing policy. KeyScaler Secure Updates then delivers the signed updates to IoT devices and verifies the device identity before each request is approved. Contact Device Authority for setup details.

**Solution Components**

- Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted with CodeSign Protect
- Device Authority KeyScaler and KeyScaler Secure Updates
