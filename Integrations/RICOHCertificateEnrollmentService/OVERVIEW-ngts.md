# RICOH Certificate Enrollment Service

Certificates protect the information that flows to and from your RICOH devices, but installing a unique certificate on every device by hand is error-prone, labor-intensive and slow. RICOH Certificate Enrollment Service automates certificate enrollment and provisioning across your RICOH device fleet, using policy-enforced certificates from Next-Gen Trust Security.

**Value Highlights**

- **Automate provisioning** of certificates to RICOH devices across the fleet.
- **Renew before expiry**: The service detects certificates that are about to expire and renews them in advance.
- **Use your existing CAs**: Certificates are issued by the certificate authorities already connected to Next-Gen Trust Security.
- **Enforce policy**: Every device certificate follows the policy defined in Next-Gen Trust Security.
- **Reduce administrative work** by removing manual enrollment steps.

**How it Works**

RICOH Certificate Enrollment Service connects to Next-Gen Trust Security and requests certificates for each device through an issuing template, so the certificates follow your policy and appear in your inventory. RICOH Certificate Enrollment Service then installs the issued certificate on the RICOH device and tracks it for renewal.

**Solution Components**

- RICOH Certificate Enrollment Service
- Next-Gen Trust Security
- RICOH devices
