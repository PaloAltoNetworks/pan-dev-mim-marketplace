# Splunk Enterprise

Security and operations teams need actionable intelligence to reduce risk, avoid outages and maintain compliance. With little visibility into the manual processes that typically surround keys and certificates, issues are hard to prevent. This integration delivers event sharing, tracking and intelligence from Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted directly to Splunk Enterprise, so your teams stay informed in the tool they already use.

**Value Highlights**

- **Inform your teams**: Give security, operations and compliance teams better visibility and timely updates.
- **Report progress**: Share how your machine identity protection program is advancing.
- **Identify issues and risks**: Spot anomalies and activities that need remediation.
- **Operationalize processes**: Drive compliance and remediation through an enterprise GRC platform.
- **Analyze certificate state**: Track the state of keys and certificates with the Splunk dashboard module.

**How it Works**

Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted transmits key and certificate lifecycle events, administrator activity and workflow approvals to Splunk Enterprise. Splunk dashboards then analyze the state of keys and certificates and the activity around them, so teams can track progress and identify anomalies to remediate.

**Solution Components**

- Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted
- Splunk Enterprise
