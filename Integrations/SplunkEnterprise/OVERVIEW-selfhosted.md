# Splunk Enterprise

With little to no visibility and operational intelligence, it's difficult to prevent issues with the manual processes that typically surround keys and certificates. Venafi makes it easy to inform your teams with event sharing, tracking and intelligence delivered directly to Splunk Enterprise.
**SOLUTION HIGHLIGHTS**
- **Inform security**, operations and compliance teams with better visibility and updates 
- **Report and share** the progress of the transforming of machine identity protection 
- **Identify issues and risks** to enable remediation 
- **Operationalize processes**, compliance and remediation through an enterprise GRC platform 
- **Analyzes state of keys and certificates** and track progress with Splunk dashboard module 
- **Transmits key and certificate lifecycle events**, administrator activity and workflow approvals
- **Analyzes activity and operations** to identify anomalies or activities to remediate 
**SOLUTION OVERVIEW**
Security and operations teams need access to actionable intelligence to reduce risk, eliminate potential operational issues like outages, and maintain compliance. With little to no visibility and operational intelligence, it's difficult to prevent issues with keys and certificates. Venafi makes it easy to inform your teams with event sharing, tracking, and intelligence delivered direct to Splunk Enterprise.
