# Aeyrix Machine Identity Protection Application for ServiceNow

Certificate and PKI management is complex and is shared between IT infrastructure, operations and security operations, and many companies struggle to connect Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted with their service management and ticketing systems. The Aeyrix Machine Identity Management for ServiceNow application lets certificate owners request, approve and issue certificates through familiar ServiceNow forms, while Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted automates the certificate lifecycle behind them.

**Value Highlights**

- **Provide self-service**: Orchestrate certificate requests from ServiceNow.
- **Avoid duplicate processes**: Remove unnecessary, parallel IT processes for certificates.
- **Eliminate errors and outages**: Apply consistent IT processes and policy enforcement.
- **Maintain a single repository**: Keep certificate information for your organization in one place.
- **Deploy automatically**: Deploy certificates through Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted.
- **Revoke and retire**: Revoke and retire certificates from within ServiceNow.

**How it Works**

Certificate owners request, approve and renew certificates in ServiceNow forms without additional training. Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted applies your organization's policy and automation to each request, including expiry dates and installation locations, and handles notifications of upcoming renewals and the renewals themselves. Contact your account team for access and setup.

**Solution Components**

- Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted
- ServiceNow
- Aeyrix Machine Identity Management application for ServiceNow
