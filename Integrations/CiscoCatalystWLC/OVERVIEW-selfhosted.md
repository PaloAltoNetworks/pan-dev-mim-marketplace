# Cisco Catalyst WLC Integration

Cisco Catalyst wireless LAN controllers (WLC) and switches use certificates for their management interfaces and services, and renewing them device by device is slow and easy to miss. The Cisco Catalyst Management UI integration for Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted provides automated, end-to-end lifecycle management for certificates across your Cisco Catalyst devices.

**Value Highlights**

- **Automate provisioning**: Deliver certificates to Cisco Catalyst devices from Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted.
- **Manage the full lifecycle**: Handle issuance and renewal end to end instead of device by device.
- **Prevent expirations**: Renew certificates before they expire to avoid disruption to network management.
- **Apply consistent policy**: Issue Cisco Catalyst certificates under the same policies as the rest of your inventory.

**How it Works**

Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted manages the certificates for your Cisco Catalyst devices and provisions new and renewed certificates to them through the Catalyst management interface. Use the support contact on this listing for availability and setup details.

**Solution Components**

- Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted
- Cisco Catalyst wireless LAN controllers and switches
