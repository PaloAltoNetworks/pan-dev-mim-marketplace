# Cisco ISE Certificate Provisioning

Organizations use Cisco ISE to authenticate users and devices on the network, and every ISE node needs valid certificates for its admin portal, EAP and other services. Installing and renewing those certificates by hand on each node is slow, and a missed renewal breaks network access.

Secur Solutions Group's integration for Certificate Manager, SaaS automates certificate provisioning to Cisco ISE. Certificate Manager, SaaS issues and renews certificates under your policy, then installs them on the ISE nodes and the services that use each certificate without manual steps.

**Value Highlights**

- **Automate provisioning**: install certificates on Cisco ISE as part of every issuance and renewal.
- **Prevent outages**: replace certificates before they expire.
- **Enforce policy**: every certificate on Cisco ISE follows your organization's rules.
- **Keep one inventory**: manage Cisco ISE certificates alongside the rest of your certificates in Certificate Manager, SaaS.

**How it Works**

The integration is a machine connector for Certificate Manager, SaaS. When a certificate is issued or renewed, the connector pushes the certificate and private key to the ISE nodes and the services that use each certificate. Contact Secur Solutions Group for setup documentation.

**Solution Components**

- Certificate Manager, SaaS with the Secur Solutions machine connector
- Cisco Identity Services Engine (ISE)
