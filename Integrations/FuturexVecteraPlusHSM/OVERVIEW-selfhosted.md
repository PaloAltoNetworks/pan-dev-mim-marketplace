# Futurex Vectera Plus HSM

Protect your encryption key lifecycle with the Vectera Plus HSM, using strong cryptography to guard against key compromise, reduce fraud risk, and thwart insider attacks.

  

**SOLUTION HIGHLIGHTS**

- **Significantly increase security **using hardware-based key protection
- **Rapidly implement HSMs** as standalone devices or as part of a full cryptographic infrastructure 
- **Achieve compliance **with current and emerging regulatory standards such as FIPS 140-2 Level 3 and PCI HSM
- **On-premises, hybrid, or cloud** deployment options
- **Scripting and automation engine **available for deployment into enterprise cryptographic ecosystems
- **Native high availability **through PKCS #11 or with additional clustering, monitoring, and alerting capabilities via Futurex’s Guardian Series 3 

  

**SOLUTION OVERVIEW**

The use of hardware security modules (HSMs) is one of the strongest methods of protecting the data that flows into and out of applications. The Futurex Vectera Plus is a general-purpose HSM that excels in the most demanding cryptographic processing environments. Venafi’s Certificate Manager integrates with the Vectera Plus to offload data encryption and key protection to a FIPS 140-2 Level 3 validated HSM, using strong cryptography to guard against key compromise, reduce fraud risk, and thwart insider attacks.

**Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted is the product formerly known as Venafi Trust Protection Platform**
