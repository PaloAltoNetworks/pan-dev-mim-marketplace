# Futurex Vectera Plus HSM

Hardware security modules (HSMs) are one of the strongest ways to protect the data that flows into and out of applications. The Futurex Vectera Plus is a general-purpose HSM built for demanding cryptographic processing environments.

Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted integrates with the Vectera Plus to offload data encryption and key protection to a FIPS 140-2 Level 3 validated HSM, using strong cryptography to guard against key compromise, reduce fraud risk and prevent insider attacks.

**Value Highlights**

- **Increase security** with hardware-based key protection.
- **Deploy quickly** as a standalone device or as part of a full cryptographic infrastructure.
- **Meet compliance requirements** such as FIPS 140-2 Level 3 and PCI HSM.
- **Choose your deployment** with on-premises, hybrid or cloud options.
- **Stay available** with native high availability through PKCS #11, or add clustering, monitoring and alerting with the Futurex Guardian Series 3.

**How it Works**

Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted connects to the Vectera Plus through its PKCS #11 interface, so encryption keys are generated and protected inside the HSM instead of in software. The Vectera Plus scripting and automation engine supports deployment into existing enterprise cryptographic ecosystems. See the Futurex documentation linked on the Documentation tab for HSM setup.

**Solution Components**

- Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted
- Futurex Vectera Plus HSM
