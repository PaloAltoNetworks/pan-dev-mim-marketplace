# Cisco SD-WAN WAN Edge App Driver for Certificate Manager

Cisco SD-WAN WAN Edge devices need certificates, and generating CSRs, getting certificates issued and installing them on each device by hand does not scale across a distributed network. The Cisco SD-WAN App Driver by Secur Solutions Group automates certificate lifecycle management for WAN Edge devices with Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted.

**Value Highlights**

- **Automate CSR generation**: Start certificate requests directly from the WAN Edge device.
- **Automate issuance and installation**: Get certificates issued and installed on WAN Edge devices without manual steps.
- **Manage at scale**: Bring every WAN Edge device certificate under Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted.
- **Prevent expirations**: Renew certificates before they expire to keep branch connectivity running.

**How it Works**

The Adaptable Application driver runs on Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted. It initiates the CSR on the WAN Edge device, has the certificate issued according to your policy, and installs the issued certificate on the device. Contact Secur Solutions Group for licensing and setup.

**Solution Components**

- Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted
- Cisco SD-WAN WAN Edge devices
- Cisco SD-WAN App Driver by Secur Solutions Group
