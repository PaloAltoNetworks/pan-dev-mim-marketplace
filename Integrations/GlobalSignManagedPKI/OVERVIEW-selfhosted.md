# GlobalSign Managed PKI

Teams that buy public TLS certificates from GlobalSign often order and renew them by hand, which leads to out-of-policy orders and unexpected expirations. Connecting GlobalSign Managed SSL (MSSL) to Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted lets you request, approve and renew GlobalSign certificates centrally, with visibility, automation and reporting across every certificate you issue.

**Value Highlights**

- **Automate the certificate lifecycle**: Reduce the hours spent managing certificates and monitor issuance to stop out-of-scope orders.
- **Enforce policy**: Make sure every certificate meets enterprise policy and that only the right people can request certificates.
- **Support DevOps demand**: Meet high-volume certificate requests while keeping every certificate from a trusted CA.
- **Avoid expirations**: Centralize and monitor certificate usage and activity.
- **Control approvals**: Use configurable workflow approval and authorization for GlobalSign requests.

**How it Works**

Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted includes a built-in GlobalSign MSSL CA driver that works with the native GlobalSign API. You create a GlobalSign CA template with your account details, and Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted uses it to enroll and renew certificates from GlobalSign under your policy. See the Documentation tab for setup.

**Solution Components**

- Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted
- A GlobalSign Managed SSL (MSSL) account
