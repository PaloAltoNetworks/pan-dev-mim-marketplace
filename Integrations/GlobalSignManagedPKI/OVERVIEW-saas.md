# GlobalSign Managed PKI

Teams that buy public TLS certificates from GlobalSign often order and renew them by hand, which leads to out-of-policy orders and unexpected expirations. Connecting GlobalSign Managed SSL (MSSL) to Certificate Manager, SaaS lets you request GlobalSign certificates through your own request policies, with visibility and automation across every certificate you issue.

**Value Highlights**

- **Automate issuance and renewal**: Request GlobalSign certificates from Certificate Manager, SaaS instead of ordering them manually.
- **Enforce policy**: Issue GlobalSign certificates only through request policies you control.
- **Support DevOps demand**: Meet high-volume certificate requests while keeping every certificate from a trusted CA.
- **Avoid expirations**: Track GlobalSign certificates in one inventory alongside the rest of your certificates.
- **Track domain validation**: See the domains registered with your GlobalSign account and their domain control validation status.

**How it Works**

You add a GlobalSign MSSL certificate authority in Certificate Manager, SaaS with the credentials GlobalSign provides, pick the Production or Staging server, and select the GlobalSign profile to use. You then add the CA to one or more request policies, and certificates requested under those policies are issued by GlobalSign. See the Documentation tab for setup.

**Solution Components**

- Certificate Manager, SaaS
- A GlobalSign Managed SSL (MSSL) account
