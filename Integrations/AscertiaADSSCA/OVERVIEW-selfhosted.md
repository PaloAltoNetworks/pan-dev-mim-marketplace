# Ascertia ADSS CA

Secur Solutions's Ascertia CA adaptable driver provides a way to issue certificates to Ascertia ADSS CA.

**Applicability**

Organizations that are utilizing Ascertia's ADSS Certification service can utilize this driver to issue certificates from Venafi's Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted

**Value Highlights**

•  Issue certificates with Ascertia ADSS Certification Service

•  Retrieve certificates with Ascertia ADSS Certification Service

•  Revoke certificates with Ascertia ADSS Certification Service
