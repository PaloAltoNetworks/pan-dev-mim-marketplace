# Octopus Deploy Step Templates

Machine identities are a critical asset for applications, services, workloads and devices to operate and cooperate securely. The deployment to a large volume of machines is a daunting and time consuming task that does not go well with modern software development ethos. Octopus Deploy automates the deployment. By integrating with Venafi, you can automate the deployment of machine identities to the applications, services, workloads and devices securely and efficiently.

  

**SOLUTION COMPONENTS**

Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted, Octopus Cloud and/or self-hosted Octopus Server

  

**VALUE HIGHLIGHTS**

- Automate provisioning of certificates needed for your deployments directly from your Octopus Deploy step templates. No need for manual processes or additional tools
- Enforce policy compliance for machine identities, ensuring that certificates are only issued to authorized entities and that they meet all necessary security requirements
- Protect against vulnerabilities by providing real-time visibility into the entire certificate lifecycle, helping you identify and mitigate vulnerabilities in your deployments
- Reduce risk of machine identity related outages by automating the certificate provisioning process and enforcing policy compliance

  

This solution is a set of Step Templates that can be directly consumed by your Octopus Deployment environment.

  

As a PKI admin, managing and securing machine identities is a crucial part of your job. With this integration, you can simplify the process of certificate requests and provisioning, saving you time and reducing the risk of errors.

  

Automating your machine identity process allows you to ensure that certificates are always up-to-date and issued only to authorized entities, reducing the risk of security breaches. By enforcing policy compliance for certificates, you can ensure that all certificates meet necessary security requirements, providing you with peace of mind that your PKI environment is secure.

  

Additionally, with Venafi's real-time visibility into the certificate lifecycle, you can identify and mitigate vulnerabilities in your deployments, further reducing the risk of certificate-related outages and security breaches. Overall, the integration of Venafi with Octopus Deploy step templates provides a more streamlined and secure approach to managing certificates, making it an invaluable tool for any PKI admin.
