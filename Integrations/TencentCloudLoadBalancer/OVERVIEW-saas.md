# Tencent Cloud Load Balancer Connector

As organizations scale cloud-native infrastructure on Tencent Cloud, updating certificates on load balancer listeners by hand becomes slow and error-prone, and a missed renewal can take an application offline. Secur Solutions' Tencent Cloud Load Balancer Connector automates this work between Certificate Manager, SaaS and Tencent Cloud Load Balancer (CLB).

**Value Highlights**

- **Automate provisioning**: Push certificates from Certificate Manager, SaaS to Tencent Cloud Load Balancer.
- **Bind certificates to listeners**: Attach provisioned certificates to the CLB listeners that serve your applications.
- **Renew without downtime**: Replace expiring certificates on listeners without interrupting traffic.
- **Remove manual overhead**: Stop uploading and rebinding certificates in the Tencent Cloud console.

**How it Works**

The connector extends Certificate Manager, SaaS with Tencent Cloud Load Balancer as a provisioning target. When a certificate is issued or renewed, Certificate Manager, SaaS uses the connector to upload it to Tencent Cloud and bind it to the configured CLB listeners. The connector is a paid offering from Secur Solutions Group. Contact the partner for access and setup guidance.
