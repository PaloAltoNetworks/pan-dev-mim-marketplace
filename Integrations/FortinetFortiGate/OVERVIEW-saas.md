# Fortinet FortiGate

For a firewall to provide maximum protection, it needs to have the ability to perform deep-packet analysis of the network traffic. Certificate Manager makes it easy to integrate with Fortinet FortiGate firewalls and manage the SSL Inspection Profile certificates used to unlock SSL traffic and detect threats.

**Solution Components**

- Certificate Manager, SaaS
- Fortinet FortiGate (NGFW)

**SOLUTION HIGHLIGHTS**

- **Streamline** certificate lifecycle operations for your devices
- **Enhance visibility** and compliance with corporate certificate policy
- **Automation** of certificate request and distribution to improve efficiency
- **Reduce threats** and errors through a uniformed deployment process

**How it Works**

Certificate Manager performs the discovery, enrollment, and installation of certificates for FortiGate devices. An authenticated scan on the FortiGate device identifies certificates that are assigned to SSL Inspection Profiles as well as the SSL certificate used by the management interface portal, then creates and associates the corresponding certificate objects. Certificate Manager is then able to manage the full lifecycle of a certificate, including renewal and installation operations for the FortiGate device.

**Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted is the product formerly known as Venafi Trust Protection Platform**

**Certificate Manager, SaaS is the product formerly known as Venafi TLS Protect Cloud**
