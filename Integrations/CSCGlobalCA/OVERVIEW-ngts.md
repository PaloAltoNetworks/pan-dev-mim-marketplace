# CSC Global CA

Organizations that buy public certificates from CSC often order and renew them by hand, outside the policies they enforce for the rest of their certificates. The CSC Global CA Connector by Secur Solutions Group extends Next-Gen Trust Security to integrate with CSC Global certificate authority services, so CSC certificates are requested and renewed under the same policies as other certificates.

**Value Highlights**

- **Request CSC certificates in one place**: Order certificates from CSC Global through Next-Gen Trust Security.
- **Automate renewals**: Renew CSC certificates before they expire to avoid outages.
- **Enforce policy**: Apply the certificate policies you define in Next-Gen Trust Security to CSC certificates.
- **Gain visibility**: Track CSC-issued certificates alongside the rest of your inventory.

**How it Works**

The connector links Next-Gen Trust Security to CSC Global certificate services so certificate requests that use it are fulfilled by CSC. Contact Secur Solutions Group for availability, licensing and setup.

**Solution Components**

- Next-Gen Trust Security
- CSC Global certificate authority services
- CSC Global CA Connector by Secur Solutions Group
