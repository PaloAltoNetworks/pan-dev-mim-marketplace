# Cisco ASA Certificate Provisioning

Organizations use Cisco ASA firewalls to terminate VPN and management sessions over TLS. Installing and renewing those certificates by hand on each appliance is slow, and a missed renewal interrupts remote access.

Secur Solutions Group's integration for Certificate Manager, SaaS automates certificate provisioning to Cisco ASA. Certificate Manager, SaaS issues and renews certificates under your policy, then installs them on the ASA trustpoints used for VPN and management access without manual steps.

**Value Highlights**

- **Automate provisioning**: install certificates on Cisco ASA as part of every issuance and renewal.
- **Prevent outages**: replace certificates before they expire.
- **Enforce policy**: every certificate on Cisco ASA follows your organization's rules.
- **Keep one inventory**: manage Cisco ASA certificates alongside the rest of your certificates in Certificate Manager, SaaS.

**How it Works**

The integration is a machine connector for Certificate Manager, SaaS. When a certificate is issued or renewed, the connector pushes the certificate and private key to the ASA trustpoints used for VPN and management access. Contact Secur Solutions Group for setup documentation.

**Solution Components**

- Certificate Manager, SaaS with the Secur Solutions machine connector
- Cisco Adaptive Security Appliance (ASA)
