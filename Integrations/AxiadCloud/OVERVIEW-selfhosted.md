# Axiad Cloud

Organizations have to issue, renew and revoke certificates for thousands of machines, and missed renewals cause outages. Axiad and Venafi combine Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted discovery and automation with the cloud-based PKI in Axiad Cloud, so certificates for managed machines are issued and renewed without manual work.

**Value Highlights**

- **Cover the full certificate lifecycle**: Automate requests, issuance, renewal and revocation for machines managed by Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted.
- **Find every machine identity**: Use real-time discovery in Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted to track certificates across the network.
- **Prevent certificate outages**: Renew certificates for applications, servers and infrastructure before they expire.
- **Simplify PKI operations**: Issue certificates from the automated, cloud-based PKI in Axiad Cloud instead of running your own CA infrastructure.
- **Support DevOps automation**: Reduce administrative overhead and access-related outages by automating machine identity tasks.

**How it Works**

Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted uses Axiad Cloud as a certificate authority over its REST interface. Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted discovers and tracks machine identities and enforces policy, and Axiad Cloud issues, renews and revokes the certificates it requests. Contact Axiad for setup details.
