# AffirmTrust

The fast growth in HTTPS requires organizations to gain full visibility, automation and control over certificate services. Venafi enables customers to automate the full certificate lifecycle with AffirmTrust TLS/SSL certificate services.

  

**SOLUTION HIGHLIGHTS**

- **Automate **the full certificate lifecycle from issuance to revocation
- **Gain full visibility** into the demand, usage and workflow of certificate services
- **Enforce policies **and reduce risk with automated workflow and detailed logging
- **Additional workflow approvals** can be inserted at any point of the issuance process
- **Automate provisioning **of certificates issued through AffirmTrust

  

**SOLUTION OVERVIEW**

Due to the rapid rise in enterprise encryption, the need for certification authority (CA) agility and machine identity protection continues to escalate. Venafi’s visibility and automation protects machine identities that are integrated with trusted AffirmTrust DV SSL digital certificates, empowering customers to affordably expand enterprise-wide encryption initiatives for more business applications in more places.
