# Blue Prism

Routine certificate tasks such as acting on pending certificates and updating certificates by hand take time and do not scale as the number of machine identities grows. The Blue Prism Connector for Certificate Workflow lets Blue Prism digital workers handle those tasks in Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted through robotic process automation (RPA).

**Value Highlights**

- **Automate certificate workflows**: Let Blue Prism digital workers carry out certificate workflow actions in Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted.
- **Cut manual effort**: Remove time spent on manual certificate updates and pending-certificate handling.
- **Scale with demand**: Extend certificate lifecycle automation across a growing number of machine identities.
- **Adapt to your process**: Expand or change the template to cover request, renewal, deployment and revocation steps.

**How it Works**

Reveal Group delivers the integration as a Blue Prism solution template. Digital workers call the Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted Web SDK to act on pending certificates and drive certificate workflows. Because the template is built on the Web SDK, you can add components to fit your own certificate lifecycle processes.

**Solution Components**

- Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted
- Blue Prism intelligent automation platform
- Blue Prism Connector for Certificate Workflow solution template

**About Blue Prism**

[Blue Prism](https://www.blueprism.com/), part of SS&C, is an intelligent automation and RPA platform for building a digital workforce that runs rules-based business processes across enterprise applications and APIs.

**Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted is the product formerly known as Venafi Trust Protection Platform**
