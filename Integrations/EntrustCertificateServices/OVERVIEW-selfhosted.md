# Entrust Certificate Services

Teams that buy public TLS certificates from Entrust often request and renew them by hand in the Entrust portal, which makes it hard to see who is using certificates and to enforce policy. Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted connects to Entrust Certificate Services so Entrust certificates are requested, renewed and provisioned through the same workflows as the rest of your certificate inventory.

**Value Highlights**

- **Gain full visibility** into the demand, usage and workflow of Entrust certificate services.
- **Enforce policies** and reduce risk with automated workflow and detailed logging.
- **Insert workflow approvals** at any point of the issuance and provisioning process.
- **Automate provisioning** of certificates issued by Entrust to the systems that use them.

**How it Works**

Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted includes an Entrust CA template. You configure the template with your Entrust API credentials and assign it to the policy folders that should use Entrust. Certificate requests and renewals in those folders are sent to Entrust, and the issued certificates can then be provisioned to applications through the platform's drivers.

**Solution Components**

- Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted
- Entrust Certificate Services account with API access
