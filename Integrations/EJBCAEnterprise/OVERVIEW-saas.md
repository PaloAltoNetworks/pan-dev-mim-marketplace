# EJBCA Enterprise

EJBCA Enterprise is a private certificate authority (CA) that you can run in your own data center or in the cloud. Certificates it issues still need visibility, policy and automation alongside the rest of your PKI.

Adding EJBCA Enterprise as a custom CA in Certificate Manager, SaaS lets you request and manage EJBCA-issued certificates centrally, and import existing EJBCA certificates into your inventory.

**Value Highlights**

- **Gain full visibility**: See the demand, usage and workflow of EJBCA certificate services.
- **Automate certificate provisioning**: Keep pace with dynamic IT services.
- **Enforce policies**: Reduce risk with automated workflow, configurable approval and authorization, and detailed logging.
- **Import existing EJBCA certificates**: Bring them into one central inventory.
- **Support the full lifecycle**: Handle request, issuance and renewal through revocation.

**How it Works**

Certificate Manager, SaaS connects to EJBCA through an EJBCA CA connector built on the CA Connector Framework. The connector runs on a VSatellite that can reach your EJBCA server and authenticates with a client authentication certificate. Request policies then send certificate requests to EJBCA. See the Documentation tab for setup and import steps.

**Solution Components**

- Certificate Manager, SaaS with a VSatellite
- EJBCA Enterprise
