# SecuriThings IoT Certificate Management

Physical security fleets such as IP cameras and access control systems depend on certificates, and managing those certificates device by device leads to expired or mismanaged certificates and avoidable outages. SecuriThings and Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted work together to manage IoT device certificates across complex environments, with Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted providing the certificates and policy and SecuriThings applying them to the devices.

**Value Highlights**

- **Automate the certificate lifecycle**: Issue, renew and revoke certificates for physical security devices without manual effort.
- **Enforce policy centrally**: Apply your Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted policies to IoT device certificates.
- **Reduce outage risk**: Replace certificates before they expire or fall out of compliance.
- **See device health**: Use SecuriThings real-time operational insights to monitor your device fleet alongside certificate status.

**How it Works**

SecuriThings manages and automates operations for fleets of physical security and IoT devices. Through this integration it obtains certificates from Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted and handles issuance, renewal and revocation on the devices it manages, so certificate rotation happens automatically across the fleet.

**Solution Components**

- Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted
- SecuriThings platform
- IoT and physical security devices managed by SecuriThings
