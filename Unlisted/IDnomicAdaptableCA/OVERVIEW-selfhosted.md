# Eviden IDnomic PKI

IDnomic PKI solutions allow millions of people to **authenticate, securely access IT resources** and exchange data from PCs, tablets or mobile phones. Our solutions also help **protect electronic documents** and enable **trusted digital public services for citizens**. **Applicability** This solution serves to benefit organizations looking to automate the lifecycle of certificates through IDnomic while maintaining control over their policy and governance requirements using Venafi. **Value Highlights** - Automated enrolment and replacement of machine identities - Seamless policy enforcement and governance - Improves overall IT security posture - Reduces operation overhead and increases efficiency **Solution Components** - Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted - Eviden IDnomic PKI **How it Works** Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted interfaces with Eviden IDnomic PKI to provide certificate lifecycle management services. The integration is seamless, enabling users to automate the enrollment, renewal/replacement/revocation and provisioning of machine identities. **ABOUT Eviden** Eviden, an Atos Group business, is a next-generation technology leader in data-driven, trusted and sustainable digital transformation, specializing in advanced computing, security, artificial intelligence, cloud, and digital platforms. Eviden's cybersecurity portfolio for digital identity includes IDnomic PKI.

**Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted is the product formerly known as Venafi Trust Protection Platform**
