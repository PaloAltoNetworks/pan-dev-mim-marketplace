# OpenStack Heat

This solution implements an OpenStack Heat plugin that uses the VCert-Python library to simplify enrollment of TLS certificates needed for stacks while ensuring their compliance with enterprise security policy. The plugin is designed to be a used in a Heat template to request a certificate from [Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted](https://www.venafi.com/platform/trust-protection-platform) or [Venafi as a Service](https://www.venafi.com/venaficloud) for a Heat resource.

**SOLUTION HIGHLIGHTS**

- **Ensure compliance** with enterprise security policies
- **Automated, secure** key and certificate lifecycle operations
- **Eliminate hassle and inherent risks** associated with manually requesting and installing certificates

**Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted is the product formerly known as Venafi Trust Protection Platform**
