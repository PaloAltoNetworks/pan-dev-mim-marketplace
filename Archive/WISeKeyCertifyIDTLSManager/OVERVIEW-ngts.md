# WISeKey CertifyID TLS Manager

WISeKey's Managed PKI services enable customers to obtain reliable and cost-efficient digital certificates without the complexity of running their own PKI. The CertifyID TLS Manager, now fully integrated with Venafi, is our platform to manage TLS certificates, which implements advanced capabilities to automate certificate provisioning, enhancing the security and efficiency of IT operations.

  

**SOLUTION HIGHLIGHTS**

- **Supports** **Public and Private** TLS Certificates
- **Supports all SSL certificate types** (DV, OV, EV, single-domain, multi-domain, wildcard...)
- **Lower costs** thanks to our competitive offering, **not requiring commitments or pre-payments**
- **Reduce risks** by enforcing policies with **automated workflows** and detailed logging
- Benefit of **pre-validated domains and organizations** to speed-up issuance times
- **Full certificate lifecycle automation** from request, issuance and renewal to revocation
- Integrated with the **CertifyID TLS Manager API**
- Advanced **email notifications**, with optional management approval steps
- **Flexible certificate validity**, from 1 day to the maximum allowed by the rules

  

**SOLUTION OVERVIEW**

Corporations using big number of SSL and other types of trusted certificates face the challenge to properly manage certificate expirations and key rotations. The integration of Venafi and WISeKey allows to our customers to automate the management of these certificates, reducing the security risks and possible downtimes provoked by uncontrolled certificate expiration.

  

**HOW IT WORKS**

To leverage this integration, you need to: 

- Ensure you have a valid partner account in the WISeKey's CertifyID TLS Manager 
- Download and install the WISeKey adaptable driver
- Integrate your CertifyID TLS Manager account with Venafi following the supplied instructions
