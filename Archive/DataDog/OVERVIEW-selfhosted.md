# DataDog

Site Reliability Engineers, or SREs, gain valuable insight into the status of Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted and the machine identities in use throughout the organization.
**SOLUTION HIGHLIGHTS**
- **Enhance visibility and intelligence** around machine identities and Venafi services
- **Build customized dashboards** containing the data and alerts that matter most to the SRE team
- **Easy to configure** and customize using provided dashboards as a starting point
- **Create actionable intelligence** from real-time Venafi data and metrics
**SOLUTION OVERVIEW**
DataDog is used by Site Reliability Engineers to monitor the health of the services throughout an organization. This integration provides out-of-the-box metrics and dashboards within DataDog for Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted - metrics like:
- The status of Venafi services, database services and DB backup location on Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted servers
- The average issuance time for certificates
- HUD display, similar to Aperture data, but made available to an SRE
- Certificate, algorithm and key size statistics over time
- Live view of common names being requested along with origins and requesters

**Certificate Manager, Self-Hosted is the product formerly known as Venafi Trust Protection Platform**
